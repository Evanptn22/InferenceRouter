Privacy Policy

Describes InFlow (this Inference Router)'s actual current data handling — see notice below.

This document describes what the running code actually does, generated from the implementation directly — it is not reviewed by legal counsel. Given this app now processes real payment credentials and settlement data (via InFlow), don't rely on this as a compliant, binding privacy policy without professional legal review before any real-world or public use.

What we collect

DataSource
Payer/account identifierWhichever payment rail you pay with — a wallet address (on-chain exact-onchain rail) or an InFlow-issued account identifier (balance/MPP rail)
Chat message contentThe messages you submit to /v1/chat/completions
Payment & transaction metadataAmount, currency, rail used, catalog model, latency, timestamp — for every request, whether it succeeds or fails
IP addressUsed only as a fallback rate-limiting key when no payment header is present

How it's used

  • Chat message content is forwarded to whichever upstream AI provider your selected model belongs to (OpenAI, Anthropic, or Groq) to generate a completion, and their response is returned to you.
  • Payment identifiers and payloads are sent to InFlow for verification and settlement of the payment you present.
  • Transaction metadata is used to compute your own spend history, returned to you via /v1/usage when you authenticate with the same payer identity.

Who we share data with

  • InFlow — payment facilitator/PSP. Receives your payment payload and requirement details to verify and settle each transaction.
  • OpenAI, Anthropic, or Groq — whichever provider backs the model you request receives the content of your chat messages to generate a response. We don't control their own data retention.
  • We do not sell data, and there is no advertising or third-party tracking on this service.

Storage & retention

Transaction and usage records are stored in an append-only log on the server running this service. As currently implemented, there is no automated retention limit or deletion mechanism — records persist indefinitely until manually removed by an operator. There is no user-facing data deletion request flow today.

Security

This service does not terminate TLS itself — encryption in transit depends on how it's deployed (e.g. a reverse proxy). Payment credentials (API keys, HMAC secrets) are held server-side in environment configuration, not in this codebase's source.

Cookies

This service does not set cookies. The demo UI (if used) stores your entered API key in browser localStorage, kept only on your device.

Changes

This policy reflects the code as of the date above and may change as the implementation changes.
